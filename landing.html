Design process

Designed Before It's Built.

Sketching gives designers a quick way to plan an app's layout before development begins. For StudentFlow, early wireframes map out the calendar, assignment list, reminder area, and navigation buttons.

Because mobile screens have limited space, sketching helps keep the interface simple, organized, and easy to understand. It also makes it easier to spot layout problems before time is spent building the final product.

Prototyping

Test. Improve. Perfect the Experience.
